# 📊 Task 2: Data Visualization and Storytelling

## 📌 Objective
Create interactive visualizations that convey a compelling business story
using Superstore sales data across regions, categories and customer segments.

---

## 📂 Dataset
- **Name:** Superstore Sales Dataset
- **Source:** [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final)
- **Size:** 9,994 rows × 21 columns

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop

---

## 📊 Visualizations Created

| # | Chart | Type | Insight |
|---|-------|------|---------|
| 1 | Sales by Year | Line Chart | Sales grew consistently 2014–2017 |
| 2 | Sales by Region | Bar Chart | West leads in total sales |
| 3 | Profit by Category | Bar Chart | Technology is most profitable |
| 4 | Top 5 Sales by Sub-Category | Bar Chart | Phones & Storage top sellers |
| 5 | Sales by Segment | Donut Chart | Consumer = 50.56% of sales |
| 6 | Top 10 States by Profit | Bar Chart | California leads in profit |
| 7 | Sales by State | Map Visual | Bubble map of sales across USA |
| 8 | KPI Cards | Card Visual | Total Sales, Profit, Best Region, Orders, Customers |

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Features
- Year Slicer Tiles — 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017
- Dropdown Slicers — Segment, Region, Category
- Reset Filters Button
- Cross filtering between all visuals

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights
- West region leads with highest total sales
- Technology is the most profitable category ($145K profit)
- Furniture has the lowest profit margin
- Consumer segment dominates with 50.56% of total sales
- Sales grew consistently from $484K (2014) to $733K (2017)
- Phones and Storage are top performing sub-categories
- California and New York are top profitable states
